Module 4 · Wednesday 21st October 2026

Project and Infrastructure Finance Modelling I: Construction and Funding

We convert the operating start model into a project-finance model by adding a construction period, a funding plan and the cost of financing during construction.

  1. 09:00 – 10:00

    Introduction to Project Finance Modelling; Understanding the Start Model; Construction Period

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    What changes between a corporate-style model and a PF model: a construction phase before operations, funding-driven balance sheet, lender-driven ratios. Extending the Time sheet with a construction timeline and the construction/operations flags.
    Exercise
    Exercise 6.1: extend the timeline: construction flag, operations start date, post-operations flag.

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
