Module 4 · Tuesday 20th October 2026

Financial Modelling for Projects II: Balance Sheet, Funding and Valuation

Completing the three-statement start model: non-current assets, working capital, equity and debt; then scenarios, sensitivities, ratios and valuation, closing with a model review against the guide.

  1. 09:00 – 10:00

    Modelling Non-Current Assets and Working Capital

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Opening non-current asset balance and useful life; capex by date and its depreciation; the NCA balance roll-forward; receivables and payables as working capital.
    Exercise
    Exercise 5.1: build the NCA sheet: initial balance depreciation, capex depreciation, closing balance. Exercise 5.2: reconcile working capital to the cash flow cascade.

  3. 10:30 – 02:00

    Modelling Equity and Debt; Scenario Analysis

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Equity: share capital, redemption, dividends, retained cash and earnings. Debt: senior debt as a share of non-current assets, repayment profile selection (7.5, 10, 15 years), interest on a day-count basis. Scenarios: the scenario switch and name, storing and comparing runs on Track.
    Exercise
    Exercise 5.3: build Equity and Debt; get the balance sheet check to zero. Exercise 5.4: run all scenarios and log the results on Track.

  5. 02:45 – 05:00

    Sensitivity and Ratio Analysis; Valuation; Model Guide and Review

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Sensitivities on fare, occupancy, O&M and interest rate; credit and return ratios; shareholder cash flow, NPV and IRR against the required return; payback. Closing review of the model against a model guide.
    Exercise
    Exercise 5.5: one-at-a-time sensitivity table on equity IRR. Exercise 5.6: peer review: swap models and run the check list.
