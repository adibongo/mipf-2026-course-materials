Module 1 · Wednesday 14th October 2026

The Infrastructure Challenge and Raison d'être of Project Finance

Day 1 follows one deal from structuring to credit committee: Project Sahel Sun, a 50 MW solar plant in Kano State. Nigeria's reference transaction, the Azura-Edo IPP, anchors every concept, and the Lekki concessions, the 2nd Niger Bridge and the Road Infrastructure Tax Credit Scheme show where structures succeed or stall. The Harvard cases used in earlier editions are retained as alternative reading.

  1. 09:00 – 10:00

    Session 1.1: SPVs, Non-Recourse Lending and Cash-Flow-Based Finance

    Lecture and Discussion Facilitator: Prof Bongo Adi
    Focus
    Why banks cannot lend infrastructure from the balance sheet; the SPV and the recourse spectrum; lending against CFADS rather than assets; the contract web; the project life cycle and who is around the table. Azura-Edo IPP against a sponsor-financed corporate plant.
    Exercise
    Exercise 1 (Workbook p.2): draw the corporate-finance and project-finance structures side by side and circle the one contract without which lenders would not lend.
