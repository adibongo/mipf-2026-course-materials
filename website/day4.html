Module 4 · Monday 19th October 2026

Financial Modelling for Projects I: The Operating Model

Hands-on in Excel with the TrainStat start model: a 20-year, quarterly model of an international and domestic train service. Today: orientation, the financial statements, revenue, operating cost and tax. (Day 4 follows the weekend rest: Saturday 17th and Sunday 18th October are rest days.)

  1. 09:00 – 10:00

    Introduction to Financial Modelling for Projects; Understanding the Start Model; Review of the Case Study

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Modelling standards (inputs, workings, outputs, checks; one formula per row); the start model's structure and timeline; the TrainStat case.
    Exercise
    Exercise 4.1: trace the timeline. Set the first date, forecast length and months per period in InpC; follow the Time sheet and confirm the period counters and flags.

  3. 10:30 – 02:00

    Linking the Financial Statements; Revenue Modelling

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    How inputs flow through the operating sheets into FinStat (income statement, cash flow cascade, balance sheet) and the model checks. Revenue: demand forecast from trains, arrivals per train, seats and occupancy; average fare; escalation; receivable days and cash received.
    Exercise
    Exercise 4.2: rebuild OpRev from the inputs, one row at a time, then change the scenario switch and explain the revenue movement. Exercise 4.3: trace one revenue dollar from OpRev to the cash received line and the receivables balance.

  5. 02:45 – 05:00

    Operating Cost Modelling; Financial Statement Modelling: Tax

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Operating cost: fixed and variable handling cost per arrival, fixed O&M, the O&M sensitivity input, escalation, payable days. Tax: tax depreciation on a written-down-value basis, corporation tax, tax paid versus tax expense, deferred tax.
    Exercise
    Exercise 4.4: build OpCost and the payables balance. Exercise 4.5: build the Tax sheet and reconcile tax paid to tax expense through the deferred tax balance.
