Project and Infrastructure Finance Modelling II: Debt Sizing and Returns

Drawdown, cover ratios, sculpting and sizing, reserve accounts and shareholder returns: the numbers a credit committee decides on.

  1. 09:00 – 10:00

    Construction Debt Drawdown Structure; DSCR, LLCR and PLCR

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Drawing debt in line with spend and the gearing rule; defining CFADS; DSCR, LLCR and PLCR and how lenders use minimum and average tests.
    Exercise
    Exercise 7.1: build the drawdown schedule. Exercise 7.2: compute CFADS, DSCR, LLCR and PLCR for the base case.

  3. 10:30 – 02:00

    Debt Sculpting and Debt Sizing; Blended Equity Rate of Return

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
    Focus
    Replacing the fixed repayment profiles with a sculpted profile: principal = CFADS ÷ target DSCR − interest; sizing debt as the present value of sculpted debt service, capped by gearing; blended equity return across shareholder loan and equity.
    Exercise
    Exercise 7.3: sculpt and size the debt at a 1.35x DSCR; compare with the 7.5, 10 and 15-year profiles. Exercise 7.4: compute the blended equity IRR (Exercise 14: Debt Sculpting as the check).

    Lecture and Exercise Facilitator: Promise Godbold
